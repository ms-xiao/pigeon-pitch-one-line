One line, at most 200 characters: the elevator pitch a city pigeon gives
to investors for its startup. A comedy judge compares the pitches two at a
time — the pitch must be on subject, genuinely funny, original, and told in
the pigeon's own voice, with every word earning its place.

**The line** (137 characters):

> We are Crumbs-as-a-Service: a pigeon startup delivering breadcrumbs to 40 million rooftops a day, and our whole workforce works for seed.

`main.ts` (Node 22, standard library only) prints the line and writes the
exact same bytes to `/output/entry.txt`, the path the kit's gate scorer
reads. `entry.txt` holds the line exactly as submitted.
